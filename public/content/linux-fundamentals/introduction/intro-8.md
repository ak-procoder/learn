## Minimum Requirements

### Ultra-Lightweight Distributions
- **CPU**: 1 GHz single-core
- **RAM**: 512 MB
- **Storage**: 5 GB
- **Examples**: Puppy Linux, Tiny Core Linux

### Lightweight Distributions
- **CPU**: 1 GHz dual-core
- **RAM**: 1-2 GB
- **Storage**: 10 GB
- **Examples**: Lubuntu, Xubuntu, Linux Mint XFCE

### Standard Desktop Distributions
- **CPU**: 2 GHz dual-core or better
- **RAM**: 4 GB (8 GB recommended)
- **Storage**: 20-25 GB
- **Examples**: Ubuntu, Fedora, openSUSE

## Recommended Specifications

For comfortable modern usage:
- **CPU**: Intel i5/AMD Ryzen 5 or better
- **RAM**: 8-16 GB
- **Storage**: 50+ GB SSD
- **Graphics**: Integrated or dedicated GPU

## Hardware Compatibility

Linux supports vast hardware, but check:
- WiFi chipsets (some require proprietary drivers)
- Graphics cards (NVIDIA may need additional drivers)
- Fingerprint readers and special laptop features
