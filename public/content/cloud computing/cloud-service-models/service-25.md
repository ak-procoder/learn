## Evaluating Service Model ROI

Comparing total cost of ownership across models.

## Cost Components

### IaaS Costs

**Direct Costs**:
- Compute instances
- Storage
- Data transfer
- Additional services (load balancers, etc.)

**Hidden Costs**:
- Staff time for management
- Training
- Monitoring tools
- Security tools
- Backup solutions

**TCO Comparison**:
- Lower than on-premises (no hardware)
- Higher management costs than PaaS/SaaS
- More flexibility for cost optimization

### PaaS Costs

**Direct Costs**:
- Platform usage (compute hours)
- Database usage
- Add-on services

**Savings**:
- No OS management
- Reduced staff requirements
- Faster development (time = money)
- Built-in services reduce development costs

**TCO Factors**:
- Higher per-unit costs than IaaS
- Lower total costs due to efficiency
- Faster time-to-market value

### SaaS Costs

**Direct Costs**:
- Per-user subscriptions
- Feature tier pricing
- Additional storage/users

**Hidden Costs**:
- Integration costs
- Data migration
- Training
- Customization limitations (opportunity cost)

**TCO Benefits**:
- Predictable monthly costs
- No implementation costs
- Minimal IT overhead
- Instant updates included

## ROI Calculations

### Beyond Direct Costs

**Factors to Include**:
- Development/deployment speed
- Scalability benefits
- Reduced downtime
- Innovation enablement
- Opportunity costs
- Risk reduction

### Time to Value

**IaaS**: Moderate (setup required)
**PaaS**: Fast (focus on code)
**SaaS**: Immediate (ready to use)

**Value**: Earlier value realization = better ROI

## Decision Framework

### When IaaS Has Best ROI

- Long-running, predictable workloads
- High level of customization required
- Existing expertise in infrastructure management
- Specific compliance requirements
- Large scale with optimization

### When PaaS Has Best ROI

- Application development focus
- Limited operations team
- Need fast time-to-market
- Leveraging platform services significantly
- Moderate to high scaling requirements

### When SaaS Has Best ROI

- Standard business processes
- Small IT team
- Need immediate deployment
- Predictable usage
- Multiple small teams/departments

## Cost Optimization by Model

**All Models**:
- Right-size resources
- Use commitment-based discounts
- Monitor and eliminate waste
- Automate where possible
- Regular cost reviews
