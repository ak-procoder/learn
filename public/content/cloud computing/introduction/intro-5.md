## Security and Privacy Concerns

**Data Protection**: Ensuring sensitive data remains secure in shared environments

**Challenges**:
- Data breaches and unauthorized access
- Compliance with data protection regulations
- Shared responsibility model complexity
- Encryption key management

## Downtime and Availability

**Service Disruptions**: Dependency on cloud provider's infrastructure reliability

**Considerations**:
- Network connectivity issues
- Provider outages
- Business impact of downtime
- Need for contingency plans

## Vendor Lock-in

**Definition**: Difficulty in migrating from one cloud provider to another

**Issues**:
- Proprietary APIs and services
- Data transfer costs
- Application redesign requirements
- Learning curve for new platforms

## Cost Management

**Budget Challenges**: Unexpected or uncontrolled cloud spending

**Common Issues**:
- Difficulty predicting costs
- Unused or forgotten resources
- Over-provisioning
- Complex pricing models

## Performance and Latency

**Network Dependency**: Applications rely on Internet connectivity

**Concerns**:
- Distance to data centers
- Bandwidth limitations
- Unpredictable network performance
- Real-time application requirements

## Compliance and Legal Issues

**Regulatory Requirements**: Meeting industry-specific regulations

**Challenges**:
- Data residency requirements
- Industry certifications
- Audit and governance
- Legal jurisdiction complexities
