## Infrastructure Comparison

### Traditional IT Infrastructure

**Characteristics**:
- Physical servers in on-premises data centers
- Fixed capacity
- Large upfront capital investment
- Long procurement and setup time
- Manual scaling and management

**Challenges**:
- Over-provisioning for peak capacity
- Hardware obsolescence
- Physical space requirements
- Energy and cooling costs
- Maintenance and upgrades

### Cloud Computing Infrastructure

**Characteristics**:
- Virtual resources accessed over the Internet
- Elastic capacity
- Pay-as-you-go pricing
- Instant provisioning
- Automated scaling and management

**Benefits**:
- No upfront hardware costs
- Scale based on actual demand
- Provider handles maintenance
- Always current technology
- Global reach

## Cost Model Comparison

**Traditional IT (CapEx)**:
- Large initial investment
- Depreciation over time
- Ongoing maintenance costs
- Capacity planning challenges

**Cloud Computing (OpEx)**:
- Minimal initial investment
- Predictable monthly costs
- No maintenance overhead
- Flexible resource allocation

## Flexibility and Innovation

**Traditional**: Weeks or months to deploy new infrastructure
**Cloud**: Minutes to hours for new resources

This speed enables rapid innovation and experimentation.
