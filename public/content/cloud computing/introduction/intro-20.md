## When to Use Cloud Computing

### Ideal Scenarios

**Variable or Unpredictable Workloads**:
- Seasonal businesses
- Growing startups
- Event-driven applications

**Global Applications**:
- International customer base
- Multi-region requirements
- Content delivery needs

**Innovation and Experimentation**:
- Rapid prototyping
- Testing new ideas
- Learning new technologies

**Big Data and Analytics**:
- Large data processing needs
- Machine learning workloads
- Real-time analytics

**Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity**:
- Geographic redundancy
- Automated backups
- Quick recovery capabilities

### When Cloud May Not Be Ideal

**Highly Regulated Industries with Data Residency Requirements**:
- May require on-premises or hybrid solutions
- Special compliance needs

**Applications with Consistent, Predictable Workloads**:
- May be more cost-effective on-premises long-term
- Consider reserved instances or hybrid approach

**Extremely Latency-Sensitive Applications**:
- May require edge computing or on-premises
- Consider hybrid with edge locations

**Organizations with Limited Internet Connectivity**:
- Network dependency concerns
- May need on-premises or edge solutions

## Decision Criteria

### Technical Factors

**Scalability Requirements**: Does workload need to scale dynamically?

**Performance Needs**: Are latency requirements strict?

**Integration Complexity**: How complex are integrations with existing systems?

**Data Sensitivity**: What are the data security and compliance requirements?

### Business Factors

**Cost Analysis**:
- Compare TCO (Total Cost of Ownership)
- Consider CapEx vs OpEx preferences
- Evaluate long-term cost trends

**Time-to-Market**:
- How quickly do you need to deploy?
- Is rapid innovation important?

**Resources and Skills**:
- Do you have cloud expertise?
- Can you train or hire cloud professionals?

**Strategic Goals**:
- Does cloud align with business strategy?
- What are growth and expansion plans?

## Evaluation Process

**1. Assess Current State**:
- Inventory applications and workloads
- Understand dependencies
- Evaluate readiness

**2. Define Requirements**:
- Performance needs
- Security and compliance
- Budget constraints
- Timeline

**3. Evaluate Options**:
- Public cloud
- Private cloud
- Hybrid cloud
- On-premises

**4. Proof of Concept**:
- Test critical workloads
- Validate assumptions
- Measure performance and costs

**5. Make Informed Decision**:
- Weigh pros and cons
- Consider short and long-term
- Plan implementation strategy
