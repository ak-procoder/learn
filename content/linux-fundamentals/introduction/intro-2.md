## The Beginning (1991)

Linus Torvalds, a Finnish computer science student, created Linux as a hobby project to develop a free Unix-like kernel for his personal computer.

## Key Milestones

- **1991**: Linux 0.01 released (10,000 lines of code)
- **1992**: Linux adopted GPL license
- **1994**: Linux 1.0 released
- **1996**: Tux the penguin becomes official mascot
- **2000s**: Enterprise adoption grows rapidly
- **Today**: Powers billions of devices worldwide

## The GNU Connection

Linux kernel combined with GNU Project tools created a complete free operating system:
- **GNU**: Utilities, compilers, libraries
- **Linux**: Kernel
- **Result**: GNU/Linux operating system

## Modern Impact

- **Servers**: 90%+ of internet servers
- **Mobile**: Android is Linux-based
- **Cloud**: AWS, Azure, Google Cloud
- **Embedded**: IoT devices, routers, smart TVs
