## Community Cloud Overview

Cloud infrastructure shared by organizations with common concerns.

## Definition

**Shared Infrastructure**: Multiple organizations with similar requirements

**Common Interests**: Industry, security, compliance, mission

**Management**: Can be internal or third-party managed

## Characteristics

**Shared Costs**: Lower than private, higher than public

**Compliance**: Industry-specific requirements met

**Collaboration**: Shared resources and standards

**Limited Access**: Only community members

## Examples

**Healthcare Community Cloud**:
- HIPAA-compliant infrastructure
- Shared by healthcare organizations
- Medical data standards
- Specialized security

**Government Community Cloud**:
- FedRAMP certified
- Shared by government agencies
- Security clearance levels
- Compliance built-in

**Financial Services**:
- PCI DSS compliance
- Shared by banks/financial institutions
- Regulatory requirements
- Audit and reporting

**Research Institutions**:
- Universities and research centers
- High-performance computing
- Data sharing
- Academic collaboration

## Advantages

**Cost Sharing**:
- Lower than private cloud
- Economies of scale
- Shared development costs

**Compliance**:
- Industry-specific certifications
- Built-in compliance
- Shared audits

**Collaboration**:
- Industry best practices
- Shared innovations
- Common standards

**Specialized Features**:
- Industry-specific tools
- Domain expertise
- Tailored services

## Disadvantages

**Limited Flexibility**:
- Community consensus needed
- Slower decision-making
- Less customization

**Dependency on Community**:
- Other members' actions affect you
- Shared governance
- Common policies

**Still Shared Resources**:
- Not fully dedicated
- Potential resource contention
- Coordination needed

## Use Cases

- Regulated industries
- Government and defense
- Healthcare organizations
- Financial institutions
- Research collaborations
- Industry consortiums

## Deployment Options

**Hosted Internally**: One member hosts

**Third-Party Hosted**: Specialized provider

**Member-Managed**: Jointly operated

**Hybrid Model**: Mix of approaches
